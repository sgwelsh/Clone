    Mission Statement

    The purpose of this Association shall be that of education and keeping the members informed of fire prevention problems as they relate to the safeguarding of life, property and public welfare by discussing methods of solving such problems.


    How to become a member of the MFMA

    Please review our by-laws first.



    If you fit the criteria for a regular or associate membership please fill out an application. Please attend the next two scheduled meetings and your membership will be voted by the members in attendance. The meetings are normally held on the 2nd Wednesday of the month but are subject to change. Check the posted meeting minutes from the prior month to confirm the schedule.
